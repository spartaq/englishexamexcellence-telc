## TELC Exam Description (`/telc-info`)

### Header
- About the TELC

### The European Language Certificates

The European Language Certificates (TELC) represent a system of internationally recognised language examinations developed by telc gGmbH, a non-profit subsidiary of the German Adult Education Association (DVV). These certificates provide credible and objective proof of your language proficiency across various European languages, including English.

Alignment with the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR)

All TELC exams are meticulously aligned with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), established by the Council of Europe. This ensures that a TELC certificate accurately reflects your language skills at specific levels, from A1 (Beginner) to C2 (Proficient). The CEFR is a widely accepted international standard for describing language ability, making TELC certificates understandable and valued worldwide.

Practical, Communicative Approach

TELC exams are renowned for their practical and communicative approach. Rather than focusing on abstract grammatical knowledge, the tests assess your ability to use the language effectively in real-life situations. The examination tasks simulate authentic scenarios you might encounter in everyday life, work, or study, ensuring that your certified skills are genuinely applicable.

Comprehensive Skill Assessment

Each TELC exam rigorously evaluates all four core language competencies:

Reading: Your ability to understand written texts, from simple notices to complex articles.

Listening: Your comprehension of spoken language in various contexts, such as conversations, announcements, and radio broadcasts.

Writing: Your capacity to produce clear and coherent written texts, including emails, letters, and short essays.

Speaking: Your oral communication skills, including your ability to interact, express opinions, and describe experiences in a structured interview.

International Recognition and Value

TELC certificates are widely recognised and respected by employers, educational institutions, and government bodies across Europe and beyond. They serve as official proof of language proficiency for a multitude of purposes, including:

Academic Applications: For university admissions and study programs.

Professional Opportunities: For job applications and career advancement in international environments.

Immigration and Citizenship: For official language requirements in various countries.
